What we are

We are the technical club of the CSE Department. We learn how networks move, how they get broken into, and how a room full of curious students builds things together. Show up from any B.Tech branch with questions. Leave with a team.

Our vision: lead in connected innovation, and empower students to explore, create and evolve in networking, cybersecurity and emerging technology. Mission status: ongoing.
