We arethe network.

Technovation is the networking club of ABES Engineering College. Students from every branch, learning how machines talk, how they get broken, and how to build them better. Together.

We learn by breaking things on purpose, then fixing them in public. Nobody figures it out alone. Show up with questions and leave with a team.

CollaborateConnectGrow

Our vision: lead in connected innovation and empower students to explore, create and evolve in networking, cybersecurity and emerging technology. Mission status: ongoing.

AWS

Cloud from first principles. Launch it, scale it, and learn why you should switch it off.

Cyber Security

Think like the attacker. Threat models, CTF-style puzzles, and the habit of asking what happens when this breaks.

Networking

Packets, routes and protocols. How the internet really moves, and how to design a network that holds.

Who can join?

Every B.Tech branch. We focus on first and second years.

Once a year, online, at the end of June. The core team runs it with our faculty coordinator.

Technical, Events, PR and Outreach, Graphics, Content and Production.
