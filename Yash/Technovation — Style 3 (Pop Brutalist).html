FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can join Technovation?

All B.Tech branches are welcome. We focus on 1st and 2nd year students.

When does recruitment happen?

Online, once a year at the end of June, run by the core team with our faculty coordinator.

Which departments can I join?

Technical, Events, PR & Outreach, Graphics, Content and Production.

How do I contact the club?

Write to abes.technovation@gmail.com or message us on Instagram, LinkedIn or Facebook.
