About the club

We are a community of students who care about networking, cybersecurity and real-world problem solving. We design networks, host security challenges and learn by building, breaking and fixing things together.

Collaborate

Work on problems as a team, across branches and years.

Connect

Meet peers, seniors and speakers from the wider tech community.

Grow

Turn curiosity into skills through workshops and contests.

What we work on

Three domains sit at the centre of the club. Descriptions are draft copy until the team confirms them.

AWS

Cloud basics through hands-on practice: compute, storage and networking, deployed for real.

Cyber Security

Learn how attacks work so you can defend against them, through challenges and CTF-style rounds.

Networking

How data moves between machines, and how to design the networks it travels across.

Questions

Who can join?

Students from all B.Tech branches. We focus on 1st and 2nd year students.

When is recruitment?

Online, once a year at the end of June, run by the core team with our faculty coordinator.

Which departments are there?

Technical, Events, PR and Outreach, Graphics, Content and Production.

How do I contact the club?

Email abes.technovation@gmail.com or message us on Instagram.
