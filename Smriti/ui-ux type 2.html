Who can join?

Any student curious about networks, systems or security. Every year of study is welcome.

Do I need experience?

No. We start from how a packet travels and build up. Curiosity matters more than a résumé.

How does recruitment work?

Apply through the form, meet the departments, then take a short practical task in the department you choose.

What do members actually do?

Run workshops, host competitions, organise the flagship event and build labs for the rest of campus.

How do I reach the team?

Email us or message any social channel. A member replies, not a bot.
